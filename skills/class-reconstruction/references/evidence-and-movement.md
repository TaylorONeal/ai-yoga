# Evidence and movement grammar

## Build an internal evidence map first

Keep source IDs, timestamps/page locations, reviewed coverage, explicit movement events, uncertain names, and user corrections. A clip, full practice, interview and public biography are different source types. A partial recording cannot establish the full opening, peak or ending. A cleaned caption is not an audio-verified quotation. Preserve repeated cues, silence and music; global deduplication can erase deliberate pedagogy. Do not stop review at an arbitrary 60-minute cap.

Resolve conflict: current explicit correction → clear class event/debrief → repeated contemporaneous notes → independently evidenced teacher tendencies → general movement grammar. The last two may support a hypothesis, never turn it into a remembered event. Do not count a generated dossier or repost as a new class. Keep direct source wording, practitioner recollection, interpretation, and newly written study cues distinguishable in the evidence notes.

Normalize a garbled pose name only when action, orientation, transition and neighboring cues support it. Preserve an uncertain alternative when they do not. A transcript name that resembles a teacher's name is not verified lineage.

## Reconstruct the grammar

For each phase recover starting orientation, entries/exits, sides, repetition/layering, breath-to-movement relation, props, optional branches and resets. A compact notation can be:

`Standing / first side: Lunge → Warrior II → Side Angle → reset. Second side: repeat; balance added only if supported.`

Use `first side / second side` when right versus left is unknown. Never replace a documented asymmetry with a neat mirror. Keep a “self-paced” or “rest available” branch optional; do not turn facilitated exploration into fixed choreography.

Preserve repeated rounds when they teach a progression: introduce → refine → integrate can be three pedagogically distinct passes even if the pose order repeats. Compress only connective details that do not change support, load, orientation or meaning. Do not hide an inversion setup, bind, deep backbend transition or unusual asymmetry inside “vinyasa.”

## Different formats require different maps

- **Power/vinyasa:** breath, transition load, progressive layers, strength/balance demands and a credible downshift.
- **Yin/restorative:** holds, props, entry/exit, rebound and quiet. Do not invent a heat peak.
- **Hatha:** dwell time, repeated practice, breath and transitions without forcing a continuous flow.
- **Exploratory/empowerment formats:** offered structure, permissions to deviate, movement/rest choices, inquiry/journaling and integration. Separate the facilitated invitation from any student's actual path.
- **Mixed formats:** label the transition in teaching mode; do not collapse yin-yang or movement-plus-inquiry into a single style assumption.

## Timing is constrained arithmetic

Distinguish scheduled slot, late start, actual practice and inferred phase estimates. If the evidence supplies 52 minutes, do not fabricate 60. Include both cumulative range and segment duration when timing is useful. The durations must sum to the stated practice length; pauses, music and transitions consume time. Preserve unknown duration rather than assigning every phase a polished number.

## Theme and teaching script

Show where a theme becomes an action: root through the standing foot while reaching, maintain effort while releasing unnecessary tension, permit a pause rather than force a transition. This is interpretive teaching analysis, not proof the instructor said those words.

A longer script must be speakable, ordered language, not analysis disguised as cueing. Give the action before the movement, keep cues short, and allow time to do them. Generated cues are study adaptations, never quotations. Do not imitate a teacher so precisely that invented language appears sourced.

## Teacher patterns

A single class supports “in this class.” A recurring signature requires independent classes and coverage sufficient to see recurrence. Distinguish documented training, acknowledged influence, teaching employment, and collaboration; none automatically proves another. Unverified biography does not fill a class-history gap.
