Field guide 01 / Voice & identitySource ↗

Your teaching. In your words.

Shape a recognizable teacher bio from the way you actually teach. Keep the training honest, the voice human, and every version consistent.

One teacher. Several useful forms.

01Studio blurb / 40–60 words

02Website introduction / 120–180 words

03Full profile / 300+ words

04Workshop & social / tailored to fit

You bring

Your teaching language, background, training, and intended audience

You leave with

A schedule blurb, website profile, retreat introduction, or social bio

Inside the method

  • Completed training vs. work in progress
  • Practice years vs. teaching years
  • Voice drawn from real cueing choices
  • Different copy for each placement

Includes a facts-ledger method, credential checks, voice questions, and guidance for five publishing contexts.

Field guide 02 / Sequence & structureSource ↗

Keep the architecture of a class.

Move from messy recall to a clear flow, or a complete teaching dossier. Preserve the side changes, repetition, pauses, and choices that made the class work.

A flow you can read at a glance

01Arrival → spinal warm-up

02Standing / first side → reset

03Second side / note any differences

04Integration → rest

You bring

Class notes, a transcript, notebook photos, or what you remember

You leave with

A concise flow map, with a fuller teaching dossier on request

Inside the method

  • Explicit side, repeat & optional-path logic
  • Source-supported vs. inferred material
  • Timing that adds up to actual practice
  • A complete speakable teaching script

Includes movement grammar, source and inference rules, style-specific reconstruction, and a 19-part dossier template with a real teaching script.

Field guide 03 / Reflection & refinementSource ↗

Turn reflection into better teaching.

Make sense of voice notes, class plans, and student reviews. Name what landed, understand what needs attention, and choose a concrete experiment for next class.

A useful reflection has a next step

01Notice / the second side felt rushed

02Locate / transition into the balance

03Try / introduce the option earlier

04Return / compare after the next class

You bring

Your class plan, a voice-note transcript, or pasted public reviews

You leave with

Student practice notes, a teacher self-review, or a reviews digest

Inside the method

  • A specific moment, not a vague critique
  • Review patterns with real denominators
  • Serious concerns beyond simple frequency
  • One next-class experiment & follow-through

Three modes: student notes, private self-review, and reviews digest. Includes templates, a review parser, and evidence-aware interpretation.

Field guide 04 / History & perspectiveSource ↗

See the practice behind the numbers.

Reconcile years of classes, teachers, and training across booking platforms and scattered records. Keep what happened distinct from what was booked.

Different records. One honest history.

01Booking + reminder → one candidate

02Check-in or recollection → attendance evidence

03Two classes in one day → two visits

04Training hours → explicit, separate evidence

You bring

Booking history, studio exports, calendars, notebooks, or personal recall

You leave with

A reconciled private ledger and a clear view of your practice

Inside the method

  • Momence + a broad source field guide
  • Cancellations, guest passes & duplicate records
  • Multiple classes vs. distinct practice days
  • Confirmed attendance & evidenced training hours

Momence, Mindbody, Arketa, Walla and more. The method works with exports and available connections; it does not assume every provider has an installed integration.

Field guide 05 / Contemplation & continuitySource ↗

A little more depth in the morning.

Let a verified sutra meet the texture of an actual day. A thoughtful reading, a lived image, and an original mantra, with enough continuity to become a practice.

Original mantra · illustrative example

·Steady enough

·to soften.

·A line to try, not a state to achieve.

You bring

Nothing required; share personal context only if you want to

You leave with

One sutra, a reflection, a mantra, and a closing invitation

Inside the method

  • Verified verse, distinct from interpretation
  • Personal context without invented intimacy
  • Verse-led reflection, not a recycled briefing
  • History-aware selection & varied daily form

Includes contemplative craft, local-day and duplicate checks, the last 20 verse exclusions, persistent history, and variation in imagery and form.

A few things worth keeping

The attention is yours.
So is the judgment.

These are open instructions, not an AI service. Use them with an assistant that accepts long prompts. Saving skills, working with files, and connecting accounts depend on the tools your assistant actually supports.

Keep student names and sensitive details out of shared prompts. This website does not collect what you copy. Your AI provider’s data policies apply when you paste it there.

The skills are free and MIT licensed. Your assistant may have its own fees. Code-assisted tasks need the complete skill folder from the repository; your assistant can explain that when it is relevant.
